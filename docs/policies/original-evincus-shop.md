## Owner's replacement refund instruction

“Once u make the purchase u got 1 day to refund.”

Applied as a request deadline of 24 hours from completed purchase. The old 15-day
FAQ is preserved only above as historical source text and is replaced throughout
the customer-facing page and checkout. The request deadline is distinct from the
bank or payment provider's time to post an approved refund. Mandatory consumer
rights and remedies for damaged, defective or incorrect items remain unaffected.

## Adaptation notes

The expanded page clarifies delivery estimates, refund requests, provider processing,
order details, site content ownership and support. Privacy wording is adapted to
the current main-branch storefront (PayPal checkout, browser storage, service
diagnostics and Google Fonts), rather than claiming Shopify operates this project.
No unverified business address, company registration, support email, guaranteed
refund processing time or jurisdiction-specific legal certification was added.
