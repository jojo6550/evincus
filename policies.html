Refund policy

Your 24-hour window

You have one day (24 hours) from the time your purchase is completed to request a refund. Use the purchase time shown on your payment receipt or order confirmation. Requests received within that window remain timely even if we respond after it ends.

How to request a refund

Contact Evincus through the contact options below. Include your order number or payment reference, the name and email used for the purchase, and the items you want refunded. Keep a copy of your message showing when you sent it. Never send a password or full card number.

What happens next

We will verify your purchase and confirm the next steps. If your order has already shipped, contact us within the same 24-hour window and wait for return instructions before sending anything back. Any return address and applicable return arrangements will be confirmed with you directly.

Approved refunds are returned to the original payment method. The time it takes for the funds to appear depends on your payment provider or bank; submitting a request does not mean the funds will arrive within one day.

After the deadline

Change-of-mind refund requests received more than 24 hours after purchase fall outside this policy. If an item arrives damaged, defective or incorrect, contact us promptly with your order reference and photos so we can investigate and arrange an appropriate resolution. This policy does not limit any mandatory consumer rights or remedies that apply to your purchase.

Shipping policy

Delivery estimates

Standard shipping typically takes 8–12 business days. This is an estimate, not a guaranteed arrival date. Business days exclude weekends and public holidays. Courier delays, customs clearance, destination and busy periods can affect delivery times. Check any order-specific delivery information provided at checkout or in your confirmation.

Destinations and costs

Shipping is free on orders shipped to the USA. We ship to most countries worldwide; international shipping rates may apply. Available destinations and any shipping charges are shown at checkout before payment. Where applicable, customs duties, import taxes or carrier clearance charges may be collected separately by the destination authorities or carrier.

Your delivery details

Check your shipping address carefully before paying. If you notice an error, contact us as soon as possible with your order reference. Changes may no longer be possible once an order is dispatched. If your delivery is delayed or missing, contact us so we can check the order and available courier information.

Privacy policy

We respect your privacy. This policy explains the information used when you browse the Evincus storefront, make a purchase or contact us.

Information we use

  • Contact and order information, such as your name, email, phone number, delivery address, purchased items and payment reference, when provided during checkout or support.
  • Technical and usage information needed to operate and troubleshoot the store, including checkout errors and service diagnostics.
  • Browser storage and technologies used by the storefront and its service providers. The store uses local storage to remember your bag and session storage to avoid sending duplicate diagnostic reports.

Why we use it

We use relevant information to process payments and orders, arrange delivery, respond to questions and refund requests, maintain the storefront and investigate errors or suspected misuse. Optional marketing subscriptions, when available, are separate from order communications.

Service providers

Payment, hosting and delivery providers may process information needed to provide their services. PayPal handles payment details during checkout; Evincus does not receive or store your full card details. The site also loads fonts from Google Fonts. These providers may receive technical information when your browser connects to them and have their own privacy policies.

Storage, security and your choices

We keep information as needed to handle orders, support, disputes and applicable recordkeeping obligations. No internet service can guarantee absolute security. You can clear browser storage or adjust browser privacy settings, although doing so may reset your bag or affect checkout. To ask about access, correction or deletion of your information, contact us using the options below. We may need to verify your identity and retain records where required.

Frequently asked questions

How long does shipping take?

Standard shipping typically takes 8–12 business days. Your courier or customs clearance may cause delays. See the shipping policy for details.

What is your refund policy?

Request a refund within one day (24 hours) of completing your purchase, using the timestamp on your receipt or confirmation. Include your order reference when you contact us. See the refund policy for return arrangements, processing and damaged or incorrect items.

Do you ship internationally?

Yes, to most countries worldwide. International shipping rates may apply; USA shipping is free. Check the destinations and charges available at checkout.

Will I get my refund within one day?

The one-day window is the deadline to send your request. Approved refunds can take additional time to appear, depending on your bank or payment provider.
